# Retailer meeting prep

One page the person reads before walking in, so nothing in the meeting is a
surprise.

## Gather

- The account's `brief.md`, `memory.md` and `reference/` (see Where things go).
- Sales for this retailer: if you use a sell-through export, load the
  `sell-through-recap` skill, run its data check first and say what you
  found.
- Emails, field notes and promo calendars about this retailer, including
  field-team emails about its stores that don't name it.

## The page

1. **The meeting:** retailer, who you're meeting (by role), date if given.
2. **Three things to land**, each one sentence.
3. **How we're doing here:** units and sales for the quarter (or the
   latest full period) and the latest weeks, the trend, and by product
   line. Units first if the brief or memory asks.
4. **Open issues:** out-of-stocks, compliance, pricing or data errors, each
   with its status and owner.
5. **What they'll likely raise**, with a short answer for each.
6. **Our asks**, each with a date.
7. **Don't say:** anything from the internal sections below.

Keep it to one page: under about 600 words before a short sources list at
the end. Count the words before you save, and cut until it fits. Save it as
`<date>-<retailer>-meeting-prep.md` in the account's outputs folder.

## Rules

- **Every number cites its file** and rows or filter.
- **This page is for the person, not the buyer.** Put comparisons with
  other retailers, panel or syndicated data (licensed even when unmarked),
  and anything told in confidence under a heading **Internal: don't share**,
  and keep them out of the talking points. Never hand one retailer another
  retailer's sales, prices or promotion plans.
- **No personal contact details** and no shopper names.
- **Only what's known.** An issue without an owner says "owner: not set".
  An action is "open" until a file shows it done. When two files disagree
  (an email and a field note), say so on the page and cite both. Mark a
  hunch as a hunch.

## Where things go

These lines fit the course workspace (`about-me/`, `projects/<account>/`,
`outputs/`). Skip any part that isn't there. The account is the client the
work is for. `projects/rita-agent/` is the course kit, never an account:
its practice data is for Dalton, its practice client. List `projects/`
first, and never say an account's folder is missing without having listed
it.

- **Read the account first.** List `projects/` to find the account's folder
  (the practice account is `projects/dalton/`), read its `brief.md` and
  `memory.md` before you start, and use its `reference/` folder. Work for one
  engagement (`projects/<account>/<engagement>/`) reads that folder's files
  too.
- **Save** to `outputs/<account>/` (or `outputs/<account>/<engagement>/`) at
  the top of the workspace, or `outputs/meeting-prep/` when no account
  applies, unless the person names a place. Say where it is.
- **Offer a memory line:** end your reply with the entry itself, in a quote
  block, for the account's `memory.md` (the meeting, the file, what's open).
  Add it only on a yes, with no client figures (any number from the data:
  units, returns, prices, sales), contact details or anything confidential.
