# Weekly client update

A short email the client can read in a minute and forward unchanged.

## Gather

- The account's `brief.md` (who reads the update, how they like work) and
  `memory.md` (see Where things go).
- This week's files in the account's outputs folder, by date, this week's
  emails about the account, and any promo calendar (for the dates asks
  serve).

## The draft

To the reader the brief names, in the person's voice
(`about-me/writing-rules.md`), under 200 words:

1. **Subject** and a one-sentence headline.
2. **Done this week:** three to five items, each one a file or an email
   shows.
3. **What we're seeing:** one to three findings with their numbers, only
   from files you checked.
4. **Next week.**
5. **What we need from you:** each ask with a date: the one the requester
   gave, or one set from the event it serves ("before Holiday Deals Week
   starts Nov 22"). Bracket a date only when neither exists.

Save it as `<date>-weekly-update.md` in the account's `drafts/` folder.
**Never send it**, and never say it was sent.

## Rules

- **Only what happened.** Nothing is "done" unless a file or email shows
  it; an unsent draft is not "sent". Take counts (stores visited, files
  made) from the files, and count the draft's words before you save it.
  Check each fact against its source before you save, and bracket any you
  couldn't confirm.
- **Client-safe.** Nothing from an internal section or anything marked
  internal, nothing told in confidence, nothing about another client, no
  personal contact details, and no figures from licensed or panel data (a
  retail panel is licensed even when unmarked) unless the person has said
  that's allowed.
- **No promises in the person's name** beyond the asks they've already made:
  a deliverable the person hasn't committed to goes in [brackets].

## Where things go

These lines fit the course workspace (`about-me/`, `projects/<account>/`,
`outputs/`). Skip any part that isn't there. The account is the client the
work is for. `projects/rita-agent/` is the course kit, never an account:
its practice data is for Dalton, its practice client. List `projects/`
first, and never say an account's folder is missing without having listed
it.

- **Read the account first:** list `projects/` to find its folder (the
  practice account is `projects/dalton/`) and read its `brief.md`,
  `memory.md` and `reference/` folder; an engagement's folder too, when the
  update is for one.
- **Learn from the sources, not old outputs.** Take the client's style
  and rules from its `brief.md` and `reference/`, not by copying an earlier
  file in `outputs/`. If an old deliverable is the best example, suggest
  putting a copy in `reference/`.
- **Save** to `outputs/<account>/drafts/` at the top of the workspace, or
  `outputs/drafts/` when no account applies, unless the person names a
  place. Say where it is.
- **Offer a memory line:** end your reply with the entry itself, in a quote
  block, for the account's `memory.md` (update drafted, where, what's open).
  Add it only on a yes, with no client figures (any number from the data:
  units, returns, prices, sales), contact details or anything confidential.
