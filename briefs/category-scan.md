# Brief template: category scan

**Question:** What is happening in [category] at [retail channel] right now?

## Scope to confirm
- Category definition (what is in, what is out)
- Channel and retailers
- Time window (last 4, 13 or 52 weeks)

## Gather
- The team's own sales and panel files for the category
- Public sources: retailer announcements and earnings commentary, brand
  launches, government retail statistics, trade press (marked secondary)

## The answer (3 bullets)
- The category is [growing/shrinking X%] driven by [what].
- The biggest change on the shelf is [launch, price move, space change].
- For [brand], the opportunity is [specific], and the risk is [specific].

## Then
Findings (each cited) → what we are watching → the ask → sources → review notes.
