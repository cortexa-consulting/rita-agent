# Brief template: shopper trends

**Question:** What are shoppers in [category] doing differently, and what should [brand] do about it?

## Scope to confirm
- Category, shopper segment, channel
- Time window

## Gather
- Field notes (what store teams hear and see), summarized without naming any
  shopper
- Public research: government statistics, published consumer surveys with
  their sample size and date, retailer commentary
- The team's own sales data for evidence of the trend

## The answer (3 bullets)
- Shoppers are [behavior], shown by [evidence, with sample size and date].
- It matters for [brand] because [specific].
- We recommend [action].

## Rules
Every survey statistic carries its source, sample size and date. A trend
from one store visit is an anecdote; say so.
