# Brief template: market share

**Question:** How is [brand]'s share of [category] moving at [retailers], and why?

## Scope to confirm
- Brand, category, retailers, weeks (start and end dates)
- Units or dollars (or both)
- Source of the total market (a retail panel file, a syndicated report)

## Analysis
1. Share by week, by retailer, in **points**. Start, end, change.
2. Where the change came from: which retailer, which region, which product.
3. Who gained what we lost (or lost what we gained).
4. Cross-check: does the brand's own sell-through agree with the panel? If
   not, explain the gap before going on.
5. Likely causes, each with its evidence and a confidence level.

## The answer (3 bullets)
- Share is [up/down X.X points] to [Y%] at [retailers] over [weeks].
- The move came from [where], driven by [cause, evidence].
- We recommend [action] by [date].

## Watch-outs
Denominator, panel coverage, weeks compared, data issues found.
