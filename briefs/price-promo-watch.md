# Brief template: price and promotion watch

**Question:** Who is promoting what in [category] at [retailers], at what price, and does it work?

## Scope to confirm
- Products and competitors to watch
- Retailers and weeks
- Our own promo calendar (file) to compare against

## Analysis
1. Our promotions: weeks, depth of discount, lift in units vs the weeks
   around them. Say how the baseline was chosen.
2. Competitor promotions seen (field notes, public retailer ads): product,
   price, dates, source.
3. Price gaps at the shelf: our price vs the nearest competitor, by retailer.
4. Upcoming events (the Q4 promo calendar) and where we are exposed.

## The answer (3 bullets)
- Our [promo] lifted units [X%] at a [Y%] discount; [worth repeating / not].
- [Competitor] is [undercutting / matching] us on [product] at [retailer].
- For [next event], we recommend [action].

**The ask:** [who] decides [what] by [date].

## Page 1 numbers and chart
- Numbers: the lift in units; the discount depth; the price gap to the
  nearest competitor.
- Chart: weekly units around the promotion, promotion weeks shaded and the
  baseline marked, so the lift (and any dip after) is visible.

## Watch-outs
Lift is not profit. A promo that pulls sales forward shows a dip after it.
