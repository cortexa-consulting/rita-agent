# Brief template: competitor launch

**Question:** [Competitor] launched [product] at [retailers] on [date]. What does it mean for [brand]?

## Scope to confirm
- The product and where it launched
- Which of our products it competes with
- Weeks before and after the launch to compare (equal lengths)

## Analysis
1. What launched: product, price, placement (public sources and field notes).
2. Share before vs after, in points, for the competitor and for us, at the
   launch retailers vs the others (the others are the comparison group).
3. Our response options, with what each costs.

## The answer (3 bullets)
- Since [date], [competitor] gained [X points] at [retailers]; we [lost / held].
- The loss is [concentrated in / spread across] [where].
- We recommend [response] before [event].

**The ask:** [who] decides [what] by [date].

## Page 1 numbers and chart
- Numbers: the competitor's change in points at the launch retailers; ours;
  the comparison retailers' change (before → after under each).
- Chart: change in share, in points, by retailer, one bar per brand; launch
  retailers first and shaded.

## Watch-outs
A few weeks after a launch is early. Say how many weeks the comparison covers.
