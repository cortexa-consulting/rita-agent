# Dalton Computers brand guide (fictional, for training)

## Colors
- Dalton Navy #1B3A5C: headlines, primary shapes
- Signal Blue #2F80ED: one highlight per slide
- Graphite #3C4148: body text
- Cloud #F4F6F8: backgrounds

## Type
- Headlines: a clean sans serif, bold (Calibri or Arial if nothing else is installed)
- Body: the same family, regular, no smaller than 14pt on slides

## Voice
- Plain, confident, specific. Numbers over adjectives.
- Never "revolutionary", "game-changing" or "best-in-class".
- Product names in full on first use: "Dalton Aero 14", then "Aero 14".

## Charts
- Dalton in Dalton Navy; competitors in grays. Label lines directly instead of using a legend.
